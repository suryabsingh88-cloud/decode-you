Questions, answered.

What is Basic Membership?

For £19 a month you get Sunday Reset every Sunday, Decode You group workshops, selected member gatherings, publications and learning resources, the Stay Natural Circle and My Space member access.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Membership renews monthly and you can cancel anytime.

When is Sunday Reset?

Every Sunday at 8:00 a.m. UK time, live online for 60 minutes. Recordings are added every week.

Do I need any prior knowledge?

No. The Decode You workshop introduces the ideas in an accessible way.

What is the difference between the workshop and the personal journey?

The workshop is a live group introduction to the framework. The three-session journey is personal to you: a life analysis, a written report, a diet and wellness protocol, remedies and an action plan.

Is this a medical diagnosis or a prediction?

No. Decode You is a personal self-understanding framework. It does not replace medical, psychological, financial or other professional advice, and it does not guarantee outcomes.
