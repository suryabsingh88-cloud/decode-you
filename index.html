The Framework

Nine Planets. Five Elements. A Framework to Explore You.

Traditional Indian knowledge offers different ways of understanding human nature and experience.

The Nine Planets

The Planetary Blueprint

In Vedic astrology, the nine planets represent different dimensions of human experience, from thinking and communication to emotions, discipline, desire and purpose.

Their traditional symbolism offers a framework for exploring tendencies, relationships and recurring life themes.

The Five Elements

Understanding Your Nature

Earth, Water, Fire, Air and Space describe qualities of nature and human experience in traditional Indian systems.

These elements are also used to understand three broad body types and their associated tendencies, preferences and needs.

Decode You brings these two perspectives together to help you explore your individuality.

Not to put you into a fixed category, but to give you another way to understand yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Decode You?

Decode You is a guided workshop exploring human nature through the nine planets, five elements and traditional Indian knowledge. It connects these ideas with body types, daily routines and practical wellbeing.

Is this an astrology reading?

No. Decode You is a group workshop, not an individual birth-chart reading or prediction session. It introduces traditional planetary symbolism as one framework for exploring human experience.

Do I need prior knowledge of astrology or Ayurveda?

No. The workshop is designed as an accessible introduction. You only need curiosity and a willingness to explore.

Will we explore practical applications?

Yes. Alongside the concepts, you will explore body types, seasonal living, food principles, daily routines, guided sound practice and chanting.

Is this a personal consultation?

No. It is a group learning experience. Individual birth-chart analysis and personal consultations are not included.

What will I take away?

You will leave with a framework for reflecting on your tendencies, reconsidering everyday habits and exploring how traditional knowledge connects with your own experience.

How do I join?

Register online. You will receive the information needed to join the live workshop.
