Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sunday Reset?

A live 60-minute online session every Sunday. It combines gentle movement and stretching, self-acupressure, comfortable breathing and guided relaxation, with a short learning segment on the month's theme.

When is it, and in which time zone?

Every Sunday at 8:00 UK time, live on Zoom.

What does it cost?

Membership is £19 a month and includes every Sunday session. You can also try a single live session for £6.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Membership is monthly and you can cancel anytime, with no commitment.

What if I miss a session?

Session recordings are added every week, and members can use the library of 35 guided practices whenever it suits them.

Do I need any experience?

No. Practices are gentle, clearly guided and come with comfortable alternatives. Stop any practice that causes discomfort.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Sunday Reset is a general wellbeing experience, not medical assessment or treatment. If you have a health concern, please speak to a qualified professional.
