Good to know

Questions, Answered.

What is Sleep Better?

Sleep Better is Sunday Reset's theme for November 2026: four live Sundays (8, 15, 22 and 29 November) that look at sleep through traditional Ayurvedic principles, Vedic wisdom and practical modern sleep knowledge.

What happens in a live Sunday Reset session?

Each session is one hour and includes gentle mobility and stretching, guided breathwork, self-acupressure, nervous system relaxation, Yoga Nidra, and weekly learning and reflection.

What is included between the Sundays?

Your personal digital space has four simple tools: My Sleep Profile, a Sleep Journal, Daily Practices and a Progress Review.

How much does Sunday Reset cost?

Sunday Reset membership is £19 per month, and you can cancel anytime.

Who leads Sunday Reset?

Sunday Reset is led by Surya Singh, a wellness coach with over 15 years of international experience in yoga, pranayama and Ayurvedic nutrition.

Will it improve my sleep?

Sunday Reset is a wellbeing programme, not medical treatment, and results vary from person to person. Small, consistent practices can help build healthier routines over time.

Make November a Month of Better Sleep.

Give yourself one hour every Sunday and a few minutes every day to build habits that support better rest.
