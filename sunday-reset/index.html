The pause we skip

You Know Wellness Matters. But Are You Making Time for It?

We all know that taking care of ourselves matters. Yet, between everyday responsibilities and routines, our own wellbeing often takes a back seat.

We may know what we should be doing, but maintaining consistency is not always easy. Sometimes, all we need is a regular opportunity to pause, reconnect and receive the right guidance.

Sunday Reset gives you that opportunity, every week.

Our philosophy

Reconnect With Yourself. Live in Harmony With Nature.

One hour a week. One step at a time.

At Stay Natural, we believe that wellbeing begins with understanding ourselves and our relationship with the world around us.

Sunday Reset is an opportunity to pause, reconnect with yourself and bring greater balance into everyday life, without pressure and at a pace that suits you.

Good to know

Questions, Answered.

What is Sunday Reset?

Sunday Reset is a live weekly wellness session led by Surya Singh. Each Sunday you join a guided 60-minute session online.

What is included in the membership?

Membership includes the live 60-minute Sunday session, full access to the Sunday Reset member library, 35 guided practices in breathwork, relaxation, movement and focus, weekly reflection and daily micro-practices, and session recordings added every week.

How much does Sunday Reset cost?

Sunday Reset membership is £19 per month, and you can cancel anytime.

Is there a monthly theme?

Yes. Each month Sunday Reset explores one wellbeing theme in depth. The current theme is Sleep Better (November 2026).

Who leads Sunday Reset?

Sunday Reset is led by Surya Singh, a wellness coach with over 15 years of international experience in yoga, pranayama and Ayurvedic nutrition.

Is Sunday Reset medical treatment?

No. Sunday Reset is a wellbeing programme and does not replace medical advice. Please speak to your doctor about any health concerns.
