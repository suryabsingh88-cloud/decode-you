The Decode You approach

9 planets. 5 elements. 1 you.

In traditional Indian knowledge, the nine planets describe different sides of human experience, from thinking and emotion to discipline and purpose. The five elements, Earth, Water, Fire, Air and Space, describe the qualities of your nature.

Together they give us a framework to understand you. The aim is not to put you in a box. It is to help you understand what makes you, you.

Your life is unique. Your way of living should be too.

It is a personal journey of three sessions with Surya. It explores your life journey, current direction and wellbeing through Vedic astrology, and gives you a written report, a diet and wellness protocol, remedies and an action plan.

Is this an astrology reading or a prediction?

Decode You draws on traditional planetary interpretation and a five-element framework. It goes beyond a conventional horoscope discussion by exploring how these ideas can inform personal reflection and everyday choices. It does not guarantee outcomes.

Do I need any prior knowledge?

No. You only need curiosity and a willingness to explore.

After you buy the journey, you choose a date and time for your sessions on the booking page. Your plan covers the three sessions, so there is nothing more to pay when you book.

No. The Decode You workshop is a separate booking, paid on its own. The journey is personal to you, while the workshop is a live group introduction to the framework.
