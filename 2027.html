About Stay Natural
What is Stay Natural?

Stay Natural helps you understand your own nature and live in line with it. It brings traditional knowledge from Yoga, Ayurveda and Vedic astrology into everyday life in a practical way. It was founded by Surya Singh, who has been teaching Yoga since 2011.

Do I need any prior knowledge?

No. You do not need to know anything about Yoga, Ayurveda or astrology. Everything is explained clearly, without jargon.

Membership and My Space
What is Stay Natural membership?

Basic Membership is £19 a month and you can cancel anytime. It brings together live experiences, learning resources and a community to support your journey. The Decode 2027 journey includes 3 months of it, and the 2027 Yearly includes 12 months of membership.

What are the benefits of membership?
  • Sunday Reset live every Sunday at 8:00 a.m. UK time
  • Recordings, so you can catch up on a missed Sunday
  • A monthly wellbeing theme, with weekly reflection and daily micro-practices
  • A member library of 35 guided practices: breathwork, relaxation, movement and focus
  • Decode You group workshops
  • Selected member gatherings
  • Publications and learning resources
  • My Space, your private member area
What is My Space?

My Space is your private home inside Stay Natural. One link, and it opens like an app on your phone or laptop. It holds your Sunday Reset link, the monthly recordings, the monthly theme and a daily check-in. Once you have decoded your nature with Surya, it also holds what is made only for you: food guidance, daily and seasonal routines, mantras and body movements.

What is Sunday Reset?

Sunday Reset is our weekly live online session with Surya Singh, every Sunday at 8:00 a.m. UK time, for 60 minutes. Each month follows one wellbeing theme. Sessions combine guided practices, such as breathwork, stretching and gentle movement, chanting, relaxation and quiet reflection, with simple learning you can carry into your week. It is more than a yoga or meditation class.

What happens if I miss a Sunday?

Nothing is lost. The recording is added to My Space, so you can catch up whenever it suits you. Because each month follows one theme, it is easy to pick up where you left off.

Do I need to be fit or experienced?

No. Sunday Reset is for everyone, whether you are just beginning, building consistency or returning after a break. You go at a pace that suits you.

How do I join the live session?

Your Sunday Reset link is in My Space, which opens on your phone or laptop.

Can I cancel?

Monthly membership renews monthly and you can cancel anytime. The 2027 Yearly is one payment for 12 months, with no automatic renewal.
